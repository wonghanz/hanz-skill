# Resume & Career Skills Catalog

Complete directory of all 26 career skills installed in the Antigravity system:

1. **`resume`**: Master coordinator and router for all resume workflows.
2. **`academic-cv-builder`**: Academic CVs, publications, research, teaching.
3. **`application-form-filler`**: Context-aware answers for job application portal questions.
4. **`career-changer-translator`**: Framing transferable skills for new industries.
5. **`cold-email-writer`**: High-conversion outreach emails to recruiters and founders.
6. **`cover-letter-generator`**: Targeted cover letters matching JD and candidate resume.
7. **`creative-portfolio-resume`**: Resumes for design and creative roles.
8. **`executive-resume-writer`**: C-suite, VP, and Director leadership CVs.
9. **`interview-coach`**: Deep interview coaching, STAR stories, rubrics, practice drills.
10. **`interview-prep-generator`**: Behavioral and technical interview questions from CV.
11. **`job-description-analyzer`**: Match score calculations and gap analysis.
12. **`linkedin-profile-optimizer`**: Recruiter-optimized LinkedIn profiles.
13. **`offer-comparison-analyzer`**: Side-by-side job offer total comp analysis.
14. **`portfolio-case-study-writer`**: Engineering and design case study creation.
15. **`reference-list-builder`**: Reference list preparation and referee briefing.
16. **`repo-to-resume`**: Translates codebases into verified, honest resume bullets.
17. **`resumecraftr`**: Modular ATS resume optimization and LaTeX/HTML templates.
18. **`resume-ats-optimizer`**: ATS compatibility tests and keyword analysis.
19. **`resume-bullet-writer`**: XYZ and STAR impact bullet writing.
20. **`resume-formatter`**: Clean, ATS-scannable resume formatting.
21. **`resume-quantifier`**: Adding measurable business metrics to achievements.
22. **`resume-section-builder`**: Section-by-section resume development.
23. **`resume-tailor`**: Precision customization for target job postings.
24. **`resume-version-manager`**: Managing multiple targeted resume variations.
25. **`salary-negotiation-prep`**: Market benchmarking, total comp, counter-offer scripts.
26. **`technical-job-search`**: Technical job search workflows for software engineers.
27. **`tech-resume-optimizer`**: Dedicated optimization for SWE, Cloud, DevOps, and Data.
